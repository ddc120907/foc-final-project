Our Team ✦ Our Mission

This website is our team's final project portfolio for Fundamentals of Computing. Our mission is to build a simple, interactive Tic-Tac-Toe game that entertains players and encourages critical thinking, and to present our work on a clear, multi-page site. The game lets two players compete, announces a winner when a player gets three matching marks in a row, and recognizes a draw. We divided the work between building the game and designing the website, then brought everything together here.
